      Actions
      
              
      Which of Mario's moves the multiplier applies to. Off = that move stays vanilla.
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
          
      Never changed: gravity, fall speed, enemies. Jump height has its own page; Captures and Cappy have theirs.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      
              
      Jump Height
      
              
      How many times higher Mario jumps. Gravity stays exactly vanilla: only the launch power changes, so the jump rises higher and takes a little longer. Applies to jump, double and triple jump, backflip, side flip, wall jump and jumps in 2D sections. With “Scale with Moons” on (default) the jump is earned like speed: 1.00× at 0 Moons, rising to the height below at full progress.
      
            
      
            
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
              
      
            
      
          
      
          
      
            
          
      
          
      
          
      Capped at 10.00×. Never changed: gravity, fall speed, long-jump arc, enemy and Moon-bounce heights.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      
              
      Moon Animation Speed
      
              
      Plays the Power Moon pickup animation faster: Mario's pose, the Moon and the Moon counter. Moon counting, rewards, saving, the fanfare and the sequence of events are still the game's own, and nothing in gameplay or movement changes.
      
            
      
            
          
      
          
      
            
      
              
              
              
      
            
      
          
      
          
      Off = the normal Moon animation. Works with the SMO Randomizer; this setting is Moonrush's own and does not copy the Randomizer's "Fast Moon Demos". Applies to the ordinary Moon pickup. Story Moons, the Grand/Multi Moon sequences and other cutscenes are not changed. Which Moons use the ordinary pickup is recorded in the Ryujinx log (“moon animation:” lines) as you collect them.
      
        
      

        
      
          
      
            
      
              
      Captures
      
              
      Whatever Mario has captured moves at the same Moon multiplier as Mario, from the Moon Speed page. Only sideways movement changes: jumps and falls stay vanilla.
